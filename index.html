About

What I actually work on.

I'm Aditya, a data scientist with an engineering background. I work at the intersection of machine learning and financial risk. For the past two years I've built real-time fraud detection systems for banks across the UAE and Oman, models that run in production and score hundreds of thousands of transactions a day.

My work covers the whole pipeline: pulling apart raw data, engineering behavioral features, cutting dimensionality, validating models, and getting them live. I care about how ML gets used in financial decisions more broadly, not just fraud but credit risk and lending too. I like problems where a model's output actually changes what a business does next.
